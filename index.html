I commemorate

Korean indenepdence activists

In this website, you will learn about Korean Independence activists.

HERE, YOU CAN GET DIVERSE INFORMATION ABOUT:

  • The Korean independence movement
  • The numerous heroes who participated in it, including Korean activists, foreign activists, and much more.

With respect, I commemorate their names, faces, achievements, and lives.

FURTHER ON, I REMEMBER/SUMMARIZE:

  • The judgments activists received in relation to their independence movements,
  • What the court's position/country's position is on these rulings.

Learn about Korean Activism→

Through this, We can develop the viewpoints towards freedom fighting movements at that time, and the perspective towards it in the modern world.

Why is it important to inform the activists to the society? What are the purposes?

There were freedom fighters who were well-known and often mentioned when it came to Korean independence movements. However, many more heroes had great achievements yet have not been spoken about in society.

Therefore, my job is to commemorate them and other renowned activists.

My choice covers every activist who participated in the Korean independence movement, regardless of gender, age, and nationality.
